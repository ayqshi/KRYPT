Help & Tips

Sending Files

1. Tap the Send tab and import your file
2. Press play to start the animation
3. Keep screen brightness at 100%
4. Hold phone steady

Receiving Files

1. Tap the Receive tab
2. Tap the record button to arm
3. Point camera at sender's screen
4. Align with the green corners
5. Hold steady until complete

Troubleshooting

• Ensure good lighting (not too bright/dark)
• Reduce distance between devices
• Disable auto-brightness
• Clean camera lens
• Try different grid density
